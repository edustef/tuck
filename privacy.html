Tuck Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

Tuck is a free loyalty-card wallet published by Eduard Stefan. Privacy questions and requests: contact@eduardstefan.dev.

Your cards

Tuck stores the retailer name, card nickname, code payload and format, color, favorites, notes, and creation date on your iPhone. These values are used to organize and display your cards. Tuck has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-operated card server. We do not receive your card contents through the app.

Personal iCloud, with your choice

Onboarding asks whether you want to save cards to your personal iCloud. You can decline and use the app locally, or change the setting later. When enabled, Apple’s CloudKit service stores card data in the private database associated with your Apple account and syncs it to your other iPhones using Tuck. Apple provides this service under its own privacy policy and iCloud terms. Sync requires a signed-in Apple account, network access and available iCloud capacity. It may take time; the app also keeps a local copy for offline use.

Tuck stores a hashed, app-specific CloudKit account identifier locally beside its card files to keep wallets for different Apple accounts separate. We do not receive this identifier. After an account-change notification, sync pauses until you enable it again. A different account opens a separate wallet; returning to the original account opens its prior wallet. If the account cannot be verified, cards remain available locally and you can retry enabling sync.

Turning sync off stops future synchronization; it does not delete copies already in iCloud. Changes made offline or while sync is off can merge when sync resumes. To remove your synced cards, enable sync and use Settings → Delete all cards, allowing the deletion to finish syncing. You can also manage app data through Apple’s iCloud storage settings where available. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove its iCloud data, device backups, or files you exported.

Camera and photos

Selecting a store to add or replace a card opens the scanner and may request camera access. You can use a photo or enter a number instead. Camera frames are processed on-device and are not saved or uploaded by Tuck. Photo import uses Apple’s system photo picker; Tuck receives only the image you select. The selected image is processed on-device to read its code and is not retained by Tuck. The extracted code becomes part of the card you choose to save. Tuck does not request access to your whole photo library.

Exports and backups

Exported JSON files contain your card data, including readable code numbers and notes. You choose where to save or share them. Any service or person you share a file with can read it. iPhone and iCloud device backups are governed by your Apple settings and may include locally saved app data independently of Tuck’s sync choice.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send. Do not send card numbers unless needed to resolve your request. We use correspondence to provide support and handle privacy requests, relying on our legitimate interest in responding to you and legal obligations where applicable. Our email provider processes correspondence for delivery and storage. We delete support correspondence when no longer needed, normally within 12 months after the request closes, unless law or an ongoing dispute requires retention. You may request earlier deletion where applicable.

Public information pages

Our public privacy, terms and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Your browser connects to GitHub to retrieve those pages, which involves network information such as your IP address. We do not add cookies, marketing scripts or client-side analytics to those pages. GitHub handles network information under its own service and privacy terms.

Your choices and rights

You can inspect, edit, export and delete cards in Tuck without contacting us. We cannot retrieve or erase your personal card database for you. For information we hold through support, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability and object to processing where applicable. You may complain to your local data protection authority, including Romania’s ANSPDCP at dataprotection.ro. Contact contact@eduardstefan.dev to exercise applicable rights or ask questions.

Security and children

Tuck uses iOS data protection for local storage and Apple’s CloudKit security for synchronization. No storage or transfer can be guaranteed completely secure. Tuck is a general-purpose utility and does not solicit personal information from children. Retailers set their own loyalty-program age and use rules.

Changes

Material changes to these practices will be reflected in an updated policy and, where necessary, presented in the app before the changed processing starts. This policy applies to Tuck; retailer apps and Apple services have their own policies.
